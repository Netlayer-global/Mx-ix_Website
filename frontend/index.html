MX-IX — India's Carrier-Neutral Internet Exchange

MX-IX is a carrier- and data-center-neutral Internet Exchange (IX) where networks meet to peer directly. By connecting once to the MX-IX fabric, ISPs, content providers, CDNs, cloud platforms and enterprises exchange traffic locally — reducing latency, cutting IP transit costs and making interconnection more resilient. MX-IX operates under ASN AS141539.

Peering & Services

Connect a single 1G, 10G, 100G or 400G port and reach every network on the exchange through our multilateral route servers, with RPKI and IRR prefix filtering for clean, secure routing. MX-IX offers public and private peering, cloud on-ramps, data-center interconnect and built-in DDoS blackholing — all on a flat, port-based price with no per-bit or traffic charges.

Global Presence

MX-IX runs carrier-neutral points of presence across South Asia, the Middle East and beyond, with redundant switching, dual route servers and 24/7 NOC monitoring for 99.99% uptime.
